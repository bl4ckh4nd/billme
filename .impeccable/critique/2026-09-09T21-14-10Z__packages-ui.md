## Design Specificity Verdict

A German-localized generic kit. Product knowledge is authored at the string layer and absent at the structure layer.

LLM assessment. The German detail is real and deliberate: Combobox.normalizeSearchText strips diacritics and maps sz->ss with toLocaleLowerCase('de-DE') (Combobox.tsx:5-9); DatePicker is Monday-first with German month names; Badge ships fixed German status labels; BusinessOnboarding knows Kleinunternehmerregelung 19, SKR03/SKR04, Soll-/Ist-Versteuerung, HGB size classes.

Then: not one of the hard product rules in PRODUCT.md is expressible in any primitive. Postings must balance, and there is no balance indicator: BookingEditor.tsx:686-694 hand-rolls the Soll/Haben/Differenz panel. Corrections require a mandatory reason, and ConfirmDialog has no reason capture at all, so BookingEditor.tsx:723-737 reverses a posting with no Grund field, and the reason prompt is forked at least five times across the repo with divergent copy, layout and voice. Period locks, DB-level immutability, the 11-state workflow, Mahnstufen: none representable.

Adoption numbers say it louder. Across apps/ and packages/, excluding this package and tests: 234 raw <input>, 419 raw <button>, 103 raw <select> against 3 Input imports, 25 Button, 2 Card, 1 Badge, 1 Combobox, 0 DatePicker. SettingsView.tsx hand-rolls 43 inputs and imports only Button. getStatusColors() and getDunningColors(), the helpers DESIGN.md instructs people to use, have zero consumers repo-wide. AccountCombobox.tsx is a near-verbatim fork of Combobox for the most safety-critical selection in the product, and the fork dropped the umlaut/sz normalization.

Swap the German strings for English and this drops into any SaaS unchanged.

Deterministic scan. impeccable detect --json packages/ui/src exits 0 with 3 advisory findings, all design-system-font-size: BusinessOnboarding.tsx:535 (2rem), :614 (1.9rem), DatePicker.tsx:149 (10px). Verified not an artifact: no .impeccable/ config exists, no inline impeccable-disable comments, and --no-config returns zero findings. The detector is mechanically near-silent on this package while it fails WCAG in six measured places. That gap is the finding.

## Overall Impression

Genuine craft exists here, concentrated in about four files: FeedbackProvider's scope model, ValidationSummary's jump-to-field, Input's aria-describedby merge, Combobox's German search folding. Then the same package ships its brand accent as the focus indicator at 1.19:1, propagated to 142 sites, and defines a semantic color set with no text variant, so the Ueberfaellig badge, the error toast and the validation heading all land at 4.4:1.

The biggest opportunity is not any individual fix. The primitives encode none of the rules the product is built on, so four consuming apps hand-roll 234 inputs and five separate reason dialogs. Fixing contrast at the token seam is a day. Making @billme/ui the place where "must balance", "reason required" and "period locked" actually live is what would make it a design system rather than a folder four apps depend on.

## What's Working

1. FeedbackProvider's scope model is the right abstraction and consumers voted for it. Keyed by scope with one live entry per scope, duration policy lengthening automatically when an action is attached (FeedbackProvider.tsx:39-43), per-scope timer cleanup on unmount. Second-most-imported export at 16 sites because it removed a decision consumers would otherwise get wrong every time.
2. ValidationSummary closes the loop instead of just announcing. onJump focuses and scrolls the offending control, then hands the id back (ValidationSummary.tsx:30-37). Paired with Input placing the required asterisk outside <label> so the accessible name stays clean (Input.tsx:15-17), with a comment explaining why.
3. The 400px behavior is structurally sound. Measured: scrollWidth === clientWidth === 400, zero elements past the boundary, nothing clipped, all hit areas at or above 24x24 (button sm 182x32, input 536x46, badge 71x26).

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Focus indicator fails WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.11 on every input in the system

Input.tsx:35-38 sets outline-none and replaces it with focus:ring-2 focus:ring-accent. Measured: #d9f944 on white is 1.19:1 against a 3:1 requirement. Identical in Combobox.tsx:220, DatePicker.tsx:114, BusinessOnboarding.tsx:197, and 142 ring-accent sites across the repo.

Correction worth stating: the rings do exist and render. Assessment B's first sweep reported no focus indicator, then caught its own error (getComputedStyle read mid-transition) and re-measured with a settle delay. The defect is contrast, not absence. Separately: Input's error state flips its border to accent on focus because focus:border-accent overrides border-error, so focusing an invalid field visually clears its error styling.

Why it matters: the owner filling in her Steuernummer by keyboard cannot tell which field she is in, in a form where the wrong field means the wrong number on a Rechnung. PRODUCT.md made AA binding and flagged this accent as low-contrast before a line was written.

Fix: add --color-ring: #0b0b0b to styles.css, mirror in colors.ts, move all four sites to focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-ring, the pattern Button.tsx:43 already gets right. Keep error focus as focus-visible:outline-error (4.83:1) and stop the border override. Also resolves the focus: vs focus-visible: split that rings on mouse click.

Suggested command: $impeccable audit

### [P1] ConfirmDialog cannot express the one interaction the product is built on, and does not hold focus

No reason capture (ConfirmDialog.tsx:5-15). PRODUCT.md: corrections happen by reversal with a mandatory reason, never deletion. The dialog cannot do it, so BookingEditor.tsx:723-737 reverses a posting with no Grund, and the prompt is forked at least five times (apps/desktop/router.tsx:347-391, apps/pro-desktop/router.tsx:354-359 near-verbatim, ReportsView.tsx:714,755, SonderbuchungenWorkspace.tsx:452,492, ImportHistoryModal.tsx:367) with divergent styling including text-gray-900 which DESIGN.md bans, and formal "Sie" against the du-voice commitment.

No focus trap, no focus restoration. Measured: from the open dialog, Tab 1 reaches confirm, Tab 2 lands on BODY, Tab 3+ walks into the page behind the overlay. #root has no aria-hidden and no inert. After Escape and after backdrop click, activeElement is BODY. Escape and backdrop dismissal both work.

Focus snap. The effect at :32-45 depends on [onCancel, open] and re-focuses Cancel on every run. Every consumer passes an inline lambda, so any parent re-render yanks focus back to Abbrechen mid-interaction.

Why it matters: the owner reversing a wrong booking is at the most legally consequential moment in the app, and the primitive offers a bare Yes/No with a keyboard that fights her, while five hand-rolled variants teach five different shapes for the same act.

Fix: add reason?: { label, placeholder, required, value, onChange } and a details?: ReactNode slot for a Soll/Haben/amount summary; block confirm while a required reason is empty. Trap Tab within the panel, restore focus to the element captured at open, change effect deps to [open] with onCancel held in a ref. Then delete the five forks.

Suggested command: $impeccable harden

### [P1] Input has no numeric variant, and the workaround silently destroys German decimals

BusinessOnboarding.tsx:726 and :780 run Number(event.target.value) || 0 against a controlled value={String(...)}. Typing the standard 19,5 for MwSt.: the comma makes Number() return NaN, || 0 coerces to 0, and because the field is controlled the input visibly rewrites itself to "0" mid-typing. Clearing the field is impossible for the same reason.

Input.tsx:36 applies tabular-nums to every input including Firmenname, exactly backwards: the one thing DESIGN.md's typography section demands is applied indiscriminately, while no currency or percent affordance exists. No right alignment, no adornment, no negative-amount treatment.

Why it matters: a wrong tax rate written into onboarding with no error message, in the field that determines every invoice total afterwards. It is also why 234 raw <input> elements exist.

Fix: add variant?: 'text' | 'numeric' | 'currency' | 'percent'. Move tabular-nums and text-right onto numeric variants only, add prefix/suffix adornment slots, parse through a German-locale helper accepting both , and ., keeping the raw string in local state, emitting number | null on blur, never 0. Add the missing hint?: ReactNode prop.

Suggested command: $impeccable harden

### [P1] Disabled and boundary states are undefined, so components disappear or lie

This cluster is entirely from the browser pass; the source review missed all of it.

- Disabled text fails AA. Measured: disabled primary 3.84:1, disabled secondary/ghost 3.69:1, against 4.5. Button applies flat opacity-50 to every variant with no disabled color token.
- Disabled Input is invisible as disabled. No disabled: styles at all. Measured 18.83:1, identical to enabled. A locked field in a closed accounting period looks editable.
- The ghost variant has no boundary of any kind. No border, no background, no underline.
- Badge open is white on white with a border measuring 1.24:1 against the page. Text passes at 21:1, but the pill shape that makes it scannable as a status does not exist visually. draft and cancelled share the same 1.24:1 border.
- Toast has no max-width and sits at fixed top-14 right-8. At 400px it covers the page h1 and the first section heading. At 1440px it overlaps top-right content. Toast and FeedbackProvider's stack use identical coordinates with nothing coordinating the layer.

Why it matters: disabled state is how this product communicates a locked period and an immutable record, a legal boundary rather than a visual nicety. Right now it communicates neither.

Fix: define --color-disabled-fg and a disabled surface token; replace blanket opacity-50 with explicit disabled colors; give Input real disabled: styling; give ghost a hover-independent boundary or a documented rule that it is only valid inside a bounded group; give status-open/draft a visible border at 3:1; give Toast a max-width and a --z-toast scale.

Suggested command: $impeccable polish

## Minor Observations

- DatePicker is a latent P1 with zero current impact. 0 imports against 20 native type="date" fields. The calendar grid has no keyboard handling: 42 tabbable day buttons, no arrow keys, no role="grid", no aria-selected/aria-current, no Escape, each day's accessible name a bare number with no month or year. No typed entry, no min/max, so a closed accounting period cannot be expressed.
- Portal exists and two of three overlay primitives ignore it. DatePicker's popup is absolute z-50, Combobox's listbox absolute z-20. This repo has already learned that an identity transform on the app shell hijacks position: fixed; inside any overflow-hidden card or scrolling row, both get clipped.
- Card.tsx:14-20 radius map contradicts its own type names and DESIGN.md: md -> rounded-lg (1.5rem), xl and 2xl both -> rounded-2xl. Confirmed visually identical. Two API values are indistinguishable.
- Button sets no default type, so a <Button> inside a <form> submits it. ConfirmDialog passes type="button" explicitly; the primitive should default it.
- Button has no loading prop and no icon slot, so every async action hand-rolls the ternary, none set aria-busy.
- No prefers-reduced-motion guard anywhere: active:scale-[0.96], slide-in-from-top-2, animate-spin, onboarding progress transition. The overlay separately flagged that progress bar as layout-transition (transition: width animates layout rather than transform).
- Toasts auto-dismiss with no pause-on-hover and no manual close. With an action attached that becomes a time limit on an interactive control (SC 2.2.1).
- border-subtle is used in BookingEditor.tsx:297,655 but the token is --color-border-subtle, so the utility is border-border-subtle. border-subtle resolves to nothing: a silently missing border.
- FeedbackProvider.tsx:113-116 puts aria-label="Aktionsmeldungen" on a <div> with no role, which most assistive tech ignores.
- Assessment A flagged the Toast action button (~16px) and ValidationSummary jump links (~20px) as below SC 2.5.8's 24x24. B measured hit areas but did not include those two elements, so this is unverified in either direction.
- BusinessOnboarding is 1025 lines, 47% of the package, and is a full product screen rather than a primitive. That is why hardcoded hex and an ad-hoc SelectField live inside the token source of truth. All three detector advisories and all four nested-cards overlay hits are in this one file.
- Badge covers 5 invoice states. Absent: teilbezahlt, the 3 Mahnstufen, Angebot states, Pro's 11 booking-workflow states, period open/soft-locked/closed. Each hand-rolled downstream.
- Missing primitives inferred from what consumers hand-roll: Select (103 raw), Textarea, a Modal/Dialog shell (4 hand-rolled role="dialog" outside the package), DataTable (23 raw <table>), Amount/Currency, Tooltip/HelpHint, EmptyState, Skeleton, Tabs, Checkbox/Switch, a Field wrapper, and a BalanceIndicator for Soll/Haben/Differenz.

## Questions to Consider

1. Input is imported 3 times and hand-rolled 234 times. DatePicker has 0 imports against 20 native date fields. getStatusColors is documented in DESIGN.md and used by nobody. At what adoption rate does a shared design system stop being a design system?
2. If postings must balance, corrections require a reason, periods lock and records are immutable, why is there no primitive named after a single one of those rules? What does it say that the reason prompt was worth forking five times but not worth extracting once?
3. The accent measures 1.19:1 on white and is now the focus color in 142 places. Would the brand actually weaken if accent were reserved for filled surfaces and never used for text, icons, rings or state?
4. BusinessOnboarding is 47% of the package and hardcodes four hex values inside the file DESIGN.md names as the source of truth. If the token authority cannot follow its own rules, what enforcement do the four consuming apps have?
5. Lite's onboarding offers SKR03 and double-entry books. What is @billme/ui's answer to "one system, two editions, no bait"?
